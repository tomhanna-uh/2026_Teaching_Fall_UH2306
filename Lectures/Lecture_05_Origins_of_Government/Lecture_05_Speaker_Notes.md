## Empowerment

Two answers to “where does government come from?”: **Olson** (empirical) and **Locke** (normative rights/legitimacy). Both matter for *why we limit* power later.

---

## Collective action

Ostrom line is the hook. Prisoner’s Dilemma video is for **in-class** show. Emphasize: without enforcement, cooperation collapses; that justifies coercive capacity — and worry about it.

**Free rider:** Hangover image. Group-project translation works in large sections.

**Security as first public good:** Who provides? Who pays?

---

## Olson (empirical)

Roving vs stationary bandit. Stationary bandit’s long-term tax interest → incentive to protect production and provide order.

**Speaker only (not on slide):** Democracy as more inclusive stationary banditry — coalition of citizens; leaders respond to a wider electorate. Provocation for discussion, not a slogan on the board.

One-line takeaway: order can grow out of self-interested violence (descriptive).

---

## Locke (normative)

State of nature ≠ Hobbes; law of nature; free and equal. Inconveniences → contract. Purpose: preservation of property (life, liberty, possessions). Rights pre-exist government.

**US founding as rare real contract:** consent, rights, right to alter or abolish — rare written instance of the theory, not a claim that every state was born that way.

---

## Two models

Olson = origin story from incentives/violence. Locke = legitimacy and limits. Conceptual maps, not street-level history.
