## Texas note

This is the live comparison from Lecture 9. The U.S. Constitution is short and hard to amend because it is mostly structure plus reserved rights. The Texas Constitution is long because later majorities wrote tax, homestead, railroad, and school-detail into the *rules*. Easier amendment looks more democratic and produces less settlement: the rulebook is always in play. That is the opposite trade-off from Article V. Students should be able to say which model they prefer without pretending Texas is just a copy of the federal design.
