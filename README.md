Lecture and other material for GOVT2306 United States and Texas Constitution and Politics sections at University of Houston, Fall 2026.

- Lecture materials are coded in Quarto for RStudio using the most cross compatible output Markdown possible. Rendered lectures are not provided.
- Other materials may be in Quarto format or raw Markdown files.
- This is only about half the material for the course.

    - I assign outside readings which I will attempt to reference here, but which I will not reproduce unless they are my own work.
    - I use two textbooks chosen by the department and which include electronic Courseware.
    - I use Top Hat questions in Lecture which are drawn from, but not included in the lecture slides here.
 
  - This is my 4th year teaching this specific course and I develop it a bit more each semester.
  - My focus is on teaching students to think for themselves and to look beyond the widespread misinformation in the wild.
 
    - I express that the biggest challenge of government is solving the problem of how to live together peacefully in a society of dignified equals. (from Dr. Chris Coyne, George Mason University)
    - I propose that this is a fair modern statement of the problem the patriots of the American Revolution and the Framers of the Constitution (as well as the Anti-federalist opponents of it) were attempting to solve. 
    - Peaceful does not mean the impossible task of solving all violence, but a major goal should be to live at the lowest possible level of organized violence. (hat tip to the late Charles Krauthammer)
    - I proceed from the definition of government as "the organization with the use of organized coercive violence which is widely perceived as legitimate." (Since a major topic is federalism, I leave out Weber's "monopoly.")
    - I devote one lecture to misinformation with a focus on federal budget priorities.
    - This semester I will be adding a lecture on the exponential progress of humanity in reducing violence, reducing poverty, reducing illness, improving living standards, creating a more open world, and improving the political systems of many countries
      as compared to the drumbeat of negativity from news media and from partisans of left, right, and other.
    - Civil discourse, respect for political opponents, and the attempt to find common ground are emphasized.
    - I strive to put this attempt in the context of maintaining one's own strong values regardless of ideology.
